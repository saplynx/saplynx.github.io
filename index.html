Business Description & Services

Saplynx is a dedicated software consulting and development practice. We partner with clients to deliver end-to-end technical solutions across the following core domains:

  • Game Development: Full-cycle interactive software and game development utilizing the Unity engine.
  • Embedded Systems & IoT: Custom firmware, hardware prototyping, and systems integration targeting ESP32, ESP8266, and STM32 microcontrollers.
  • Technical Consulting: System architecture design, project roadmapping, and technical feasibility assessments.

Policies & Terms of Service

To ensure transparency and compliance, please review our operational policies below.

Payment Terms & Currency

  • Transaction Currency: All transactions, invoices, and quotes are processed in Indian Rupees (INR) unless explicitly stated otherwise in the client contract.
  • Payment Schedule: Software consulting and development are billed on a milestone basis. A standard advance payment is required to initiate development, with the remaining balance tied to specific, agreed-upon deliverables.

Refund & Replacement Policy

  • Due to the customized nature of software development and consulting, we do not offer direct refunds for completed and approved milestones.
  • If a delivered build or embedded prototype fails to meet the documented specifications outlined in our initial agreement, we will provide necessary revisions and code replacements at no additional cost within the agreed User Acceptance Testing (UAT) period.

Cancellation Policy

  • Clients may cancel ongoing services at any time. Upon cancellation, all work completed up to that date will be handed over to the client.
  • Any advance payments covering uncompleted future milestones will be refunded on a pro-rata basis within 7-10 business days. Payments for already completed and approved milestones are non-refundable.

Privacy Policy

  • We collect minimal client data (name, email, phone number, and project specifications) solely for the purpose of communication, billing, and project execution.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties. All proprietary project information, source code, and hardware designs remain strictly confidential.

Legal & Export Restrictions

  • Saplynx complies with all local software distribution and export regulations.
  • Our services do not involve the sale of restricted goods (e.g., alcohol, healthcare, finance).
  • Clients are responsible for ensuring that the final deployed software complies with the regulatory requirements of their specific target industry and region.

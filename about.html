About Saplynx

Driven by engineering excellence.

Our Mission

Saplynx was founded on the principle that exceptional software requires a deep understanding of the entire technological stack. We operate at the intersection of high-level interactive design and low-level hardware communication.

Our Background

Our team carries distinguished certifications, including Unity Associate Game Developer credentials, demonstrating a proven track record in interactive logic. We continuously push the boundaries of embedded systems development, specializing in ESP32, ESP8266, and STM32 architectures to bring physical hardware prototypes to life.

Location

While we serve clients globally, our operational base is rooted in Pune, Maharashtra—one of India's leading technology hubs. This strategic positioning allows us to leverage top-tier technical talent and stay at the forefront of the engineering landscape.

// compliance protocols

operational policies

transparent terms of service, aligned with global payment compliance.

layer 01

payment vectors & currency

  • transaction currency: all transactions and invoices are processed in indian rupees (inr).
  • milestone logic: software consulting is billed via milestones. an advance payment initiates development; the balance is tied to verifiable deliverables.
layer 02

replacement protocols

  • refunds: due to the highly custom nature of code and architecture, we do not offer direct refunds for approved and completed milestones.
  • replacements: if a delivered build or embedded prototype fails documented specifications, we provide necessary code revisions at no cost during the user acceptance testing (uat) window.
layer 03

termination states

  • cancellation: clients may terminate ongoing services anytime. completed work is immediately handed over.
  • pro-rata refunds: advance payments covering uncompleted future milestones will be refunded on a pro-rata basis within 7-10 business days.
layer 04

data sovereignty

  • privacy: we collect only essential client nodes (name, email, secure comms, project specs) for billing and execution.
  • confidentiality: we do not sell or route your data to third-party networks. proprietary source code and hardware schematics remain strictly confidential.
layer 05

regulatory alignment

  • export & legality: saplynx complies with local software distribution laws. we do not facilitate restricted goods (e.g., alcohol, healthcare, finance).
  • client responsibility: clients ensure their final deployed infrastructure complies with target industry regulations.

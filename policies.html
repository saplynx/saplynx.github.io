operational policies

clear, transparent framework documentation aligned with compliance guidelines.

[layer_01] payment terms & currency

  • - transaction currency: all standard development estimates, software consulting quotes, and payment invoices are calculated and executed in indian rupees (inr).
  • - milestone schedules: development sprints are managed on discrete project phases. an initial advance payment initializes engineering blocks; remaining fees are tied explicitly to deliverable confirmations.

[layer_02] code adjustments & replacements

  • - refund rules: due to the highly custom, tailored nature of proprietary engineering architectures and consultation models, we do not issue direct cash refunds for verified and finished milestones.
  • - replacements: if an interactive framework or firmware compilation deviates from the initial scoped constraints, we deploy immediate, cost-free engineering revisions during the designated verification window.

[layer_03] project termination contracts

  • - cancellation: users retain absolute autonomy to stop development vectors at any point. upon formal cancellation request, all finalized codebases are compiled and transmitted to the client.
  • - pro-rata execution: any advances remaining for unstarted development blocks will be systematically returned to the source payment array within 7-10 standard processing days.

[layer_04] data sovereignty arrays

  • - privacy terms: we collect only critical identifier strings (client name, verified email relays, secure communication lines) strictly required to maintain development loops and financial compliance records.
  • - security protocols: we never trade or leak database logs to external advertising channels. structural source files, schematic wireframes, and database maps are kept fully confidential.

[layer_05] industry legal boundaries

  • - export compliance: saplynx operates strictly within local legal guidelines. we do not build software or firmware vectors servicing restricted industries (e.g., alcohol, healthcare frameworks, unvetted speculative finance).
  • - deployment liability: integration partners assume full legal ownership to ensure final deployed applications align with regional regulatory standards.
